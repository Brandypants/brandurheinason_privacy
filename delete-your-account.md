# Account Deletion Request for FreshPaws

At **FreshPaws**, we respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring that you have control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, follow the steps below:

## Steps to Request Account Deletion

### 1. Submit a Request
Email us at **brandurdeveloper@gmail.com** with the subject line: **"Account Deletion Request"**.

### 2. Verification
In your email, include the email address or username associated with your account. We may reach out for additional verification to ensure the security of your data.

### 3. Confirmation
Once we verify your request, we will delete your account and all associated data within **14 days**. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is completed.

## Data Deletion Details

When you request an account deletion:

### Deleted Data
- Your account information (name, email, profile details).  
- Swipe and match history.  
- Any other personal data associated with your account.

### Retained Data
In compliance with legal obligations and to resolve potential disputes, some data may be retained for up to **90 days** after deletion. This includes:  
- Transaction history (if applicable).  
- Customer support communications.

After this retention period, all retained data will be permanently deleted from our servers.
