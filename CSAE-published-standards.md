# CSAE Published Standards Compliance for FreshPaws

At **FreshPaws**, we are committed to adhering to recognized standards and best practices for security, privacy, and accessibility. Below is a summary of the key standards our app complies with to ensure the best experience for our users.

## Privacy Compliance

FreshPaws aligns with the following privacy-related standards:

- **CSA PIPEDA Compliance**  
  FreshPaws is compliant with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) to safeguard the privacy of personal information collected and processed within Canada.  
  For more details, visit [PIPEDA Guidelines](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/).

- **ISO/IEC 27001**  
  Our app follows the standards for implementing an Information Security Management System (ISMS) to protect user data.

- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**  
  FreshPaws is GDPR-compliant for users in the EU, ensuring data is collected, processed, and stored with their explicit consent.

## Security Standards

FreshPaws is developed with a focus on protecting user data and providing a secure platform:

- **OWASP Mobile Security Guidelines**  
  We adhere to OWASP's recommendations for secure app design and data handling.  
  For more information, visit [OWASP Mobile Security](https://owasp.org/www-project-mobile-security-testing-guide/).

- **TLS Encryption**  
  All data transfers between the app and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

## Accessibility Standards

We strive to make FreshPaws accessible to all users by following recognized accessibility standards:

- **WCAG 2.1 (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines)**  
  FreshPaws complies with WCAG 2.1 Level AA, ensuring accessibility for individuals with disabilities.  
  For details, visit [WCAG Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/).

- **Canadian Standards Association Accessibility Standards**  
  FreshPaws implements guidelines outlined in CSA B651-18, focusing on accessible digital experiences for all Canadians.

## Additional Information

FreshPaws is committed to regularly updating and enhancing its compliance with relevant standards as they evolve. For questions or feedback regarding our compliance efforts, please contact us at **support@freshpaws.com**.
