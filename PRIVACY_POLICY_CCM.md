# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 2026**

## Overview

Classic Car Meetup ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information:** display name and email address when you register.

- **Car information:** make, model, year, color, engine specs, country of origin, and photos you choose to upload.

- **Location data:** your approximate city or GPS coordinates, used to show nearby events. Location is only collected when you explicitly set it in the app.

- **Event data:** events you create or attend, including dates, locations, and descriptions.

- **Device data:** FCM token for push notifications. We do not collect any other device identifiers.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide the app's core features: profiles, car listings, and event discovery.

- To send push notifications about events you attend or create.

- To show your cars and profile to other users of the app.

We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore, Firebase Auth, and Firebase Storage), which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Photos are stored in Firebase Storage. For more information, see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Location Data

Location is used solely to default the event map to your area and to help others find local events. We store only a latitude/longitude pair and a geohash. We do not track your location in the background.

## Your Rights

- You can update or delete your car listings and profile information at any time within the app.

- You can delete your account by contacting us (see below), which will remove your personal data.

- You can withdraw location permission at any time via your device settings.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app.
