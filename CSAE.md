# FreshPaws Child Safety Standards Policy

At **FreshPaws**, we prioritize the safety and well-being of all users on our platform, especially children. We are committed to adhering to Google Play’s Child Safety Standards Policy and to combating child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

## Overview of Our Standards

FreshPaws complies with the following requirements as outlined in the Child Safety Standards Policy:

### 1. Published Standards Against CSAE
We have implemented and enforce the following measures to address and prevent child sexual abuse and exploitation:

- Strict content moderation to detect and remove inappropriate or harmful content.
- Prohibition of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any activity that promotes exploitation.
- Permanent banning of users who violate our child safety policies.

### 2. In-App Mechanism for User Feedback
We provide an in-app reporting feature, allowing users to:

- Report inappropriate behavior or content.
- Flag profiles or messages that violate our guidelines.

Reports are reviewed promptly, and appropriate actions are taken to maintain the safety of the platform.

### 3. Addressing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
We actively work to detect, address, and prevent the dissemination of CSAM by:

- Reporting identified CSAM to appropriate law enforcement agencies and relevant organizations such as the **National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC)**.

### 4. Compliance with Child Safety Laws
FreshPaws complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including:

- **COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)** in the United States.
- International laws governing child protection and privacy.

## Additional Safety Measures

In addition to the above requirements, FreshPaws implements the following:

- **Age Restrictions:**  
  FreshPaws is not intended for users under 18 years of age. Age verification measures are in place to restrict access to minors.

- **Community Guidelines:**  
  All users must adhere to our [Community Guidelines](#) to ensure a safe and respectful environment.

- **User Education:**  
  We educate our users on recognizing and reporting inappropriate behavior or content through in-app prompts and support documentation.

## Reporting and Feedback

If you encounter any content or activity that violates our child safety standards, please report it immediately through the app or contact us at **brandurdeveloper@gmail.com**.
